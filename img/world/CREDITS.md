# Emmavengers World art credits
- Letter, animal, counting, food, place and prayer pictures: original 3D Pixar-style artwork made for Emmavengers World with Grok Bot image generation (October 2026), background removed with rembg and resized in tools/world_art/process_pixar.py. The starting prompts are in tools/world_art/pixar_jobs.json (final prompts were tuned for no faces on objects). Owned by Emmavengers / Cletus Emmanuel.
- Small decorations inside the land map backgrounds (the .jpg maps made by tools/world_art/make_maps.py): Microsoft Fluent Emoji (3D style), https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji, MIT License, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation (full text: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji/blob/main/LICENSE). The letter, animal, counting, food, place and prayer pictures no longer use it.
- Flags: drawn from the official national flag designs (public domain) and shaded in tools/world_art/make_cards.py.
- Map art: composed in tools/world_art/make_maps.py (Africa outline from Natural Earth, public domain).
- Sign font: Luckiest Guy by Astigmatic, Apache License 2.0 (fonts/).
